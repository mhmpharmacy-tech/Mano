# Five-team article sprint — draft only
Two standalone educational pages with linked primary-source references and local brand images. The underlying warning helper distinguishes under 3 months, 3–6 months, and older children. An urgent symptom overrides age and temperature. A 39°C threshold in the middle age group prompts immediate contact for rapid medical assessment; it does not automatically assign the emergency telephone link absent a selected emergency sign.
- Visual: shared editorial components, age/label cards, readable typography, no new image dependencies.
- Code: deterministic age-boundary tests, browser flows, responsive QA and axe on the new pages.
- Context/content: date of source consultation recorded; human clinician review intentionally null. No dosing instructions or dose calculator. Guidance from NICE and US AAP/FDA is not Iran-specific product labeling.
- SEO: descriptive individual titles, unique descriptions, internal links and noindex; no invented review credentials/canonical hosts or sitemap.
- UI/UX: emergency actions first, simple article navigation, teach-back, medication safety checklist (not an authorization or actual clinical check).
Remaining: signed pediatric/clinical review, Iran-local clinical referral and medication labeling check, real family/device testing, asset compression and hosted network testing. Do not deploy or merge this draft as a public health product.

Privacy fallback: the main warning-form submit button starts disabled, is enabled after the local handler and warning logic are available, and CSP blocks native form submissions. A no-JavaScript notice links to the static educational page. Browser tests cover this fallback. This does not replace a hosted privacy/security audit.
